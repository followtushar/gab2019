The format

One technology. One expert. Twelve reasons why.

12 Reasons Why with Tech Experts is your go-to podcast for everything Microsoft technology. Candid conversations with MVPs, developers and IT pros on why Microsoft's ecosystem is reshaping the tech landscape - with insights, tips and stories to power your career and your projects.

  1. 01

    Pick the technology

    Every episode zooms in on a single Microsoft technology - from Azure Arc to Intune.

  2. 02

    Bring the expert

    A practitioner who builds, runs and teaches it in the real world joins the conversation.

  3. 03

    Make the case in twelve

    Twelve clear reasons, straight talk and practical takeaways for your next project.
